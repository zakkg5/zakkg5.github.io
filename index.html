How I work

Communication

The same drawing has to work for two people.

I made the decks clients picked a style from — and stood on site where that same drawing turns into instructions for the tradesmen. Learning to speak both ways was most of the job.

同一張圖要對兩種人說話。我做客戶簡報讓他們選風格, 也在工地跟師傅溝通 —— 那張圖到現場就變成施工指示。 學會兩邊都講得通,是實習裡最主要的事。

Problem solving

Some drawings had the wrong dimensions.

Finding and fixing them was my job. I also went through the material books end to end — kitchen and bath fittings, boards, laminates, engineered stone — and logged the brand and year of each, so the studio could look a material up instead of hunting for it.

有些案子的尺寸有錯,我負責找出來改掉。 我也把材料書整本整理過 —— 從廚電、浴室設備到板材、美耐板、岩板 —— 把每一本的品牌和年分登起來,讓同事用「查」的,不是用「找」的。

Hands on

The boss told me to install the ventilation fan myself.

So I measured it, wired it, drove the screws, and cut the window opening wider to make it fit. It runs. That is still the thing I am most pleased with.

老闆叫我自己裝通風機。我自己量尺寸、接電線、鎖螺絲, 把窗戶開口挖大讓它裝得進去。它會轉 —— 那是我到現在最有成就感的一件事。
