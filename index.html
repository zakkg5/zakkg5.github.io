02

How I work

Problems & fixes

The first thing I drew was not the shape.

A dynamic screen and a link to an emergency centre both cost money and both have to survive weather — so I started with a lift mechanism and a set of modules, and split the display into four pieces. Later problem: a black pole is hard to see at night. We merged it with the streetlight.

紅綠燈會有動態螢幕、藍芽連線應變中心的功能, 成本一定會高,也考慮防水材質。於是製作升降系統配合模組組裝, 去減少成本、加速安裝與拆卸,修繕也會更快。 面板分成四塊組合,正反各兩塊,上方放置三色燈條。 還結合路燈,避免黑色在夜晚不易看見的問題。

Working together

Each of us owned a piece, and edited each other’s.

Three students and one advisor. I had the signal itself; the emergency centre was someone else’s. We reviewed each other’s parts and changed them. This board is the version we entered for iF and Red Dot — all three names on it.

配合紅綠燈的應變中心外型設計。 組員三人與指導教授互相討論整體負責的項目,互相給予想法並更改。 因為時間因素,以這一版去參加紅點與 iF —— 這張是參賽的圖片檔,整體在訴說計畫的運作與產品的功能。

Hands on

I finish things myself.

The printed parts arrived rough, layer lines still on them. About a week sanding by hand at a paint shop near school, then again after spraying. On an internship I was told to fit a ventilation fan — I measured it, wired it, drove the screws, and cut the opening wider to make it fit. It runs. The display cabinet was ours too, built at a woodwork and paint shop.

新型號誌是找大陸一家學長推薦的 3D 列印公司, 寄過來耗時兩週。拿到成品後自己在學校附近的噴漆工廠用手打磨大約一週, 再給工廠的大哥噴漆,噴完繼續打磨才完成。 實習的時候老闆叫我自己裝通風機,我就自己量大小、連接電線、鎖螺絲, 然後挖大窗戶開口去裝 —— 那是我蠻有成就感的事情。 展櫃也是我們自己的,在漆木工那邊做的,不是叫現成的。

Shown

Shown at two exhibitions.

The Young Designers’ Exhibition and the Youth Design Festival — the finished signal on a stand, with the boards behind it.

作品在新一代設計展與青春設計節展出。 青春設計節有學生與教授們的大合照, 也拍了我跟組員還有我們作品的合照。

02

On site

Drawings

My first whole case, drawn end to end.

A three-storey townhouse. The TV unit kept changing until it ended up floating off the floor against a curved wall; the fridge cabinet was rebuilt to the size the client asked for. A drawing only works if the sizes match what you can actually buy.

去年在室內設計公司畫的立面圖。 第一次開始畫整個案子的圖面到施作與安排工種,是個三層樓的透天裝潢。 當時一直更改電視下系統櫃的設計與尺寸,之後櫃子做了浮空的設計, 搭配左邊曲面的電視牆;客戶要求冰箱尺寸,在對應冰箱櫃的寬度去做調整。

On site

Then I followed the drawings onto site.

Same case, from paper through to build — including scheduling which trade came in when. It is the part of the job you cannot learn from a drawing: what is buildable, what has to move, and who needs to know.

第一次開始畫整個案子的圖面到施作與安排工種。 當時假日睡覺都會被師父打電話叫醒。

Keeping people posted

The same photo has to work for two people.

I labelled the site-progress photos and sent them out — to the owner, who is not there, and to each trade, who needs to know what the last one left behind. The words on these are mine.

要聯絡給業主與各師傅的案場進度照。 照片上的標註是我自己寫的 —— 業主不在現場,師傅要知道上一個工種做到哪。
