New Era Traffic System 新型態交通號誌

A traffic signal with a dynamic display and a link to an emergency centre — which makes it expensive, and awkward to service on a live road. My part was the signal itself: how to build it so one crew can put it up, open it and swap a part without closing the junction. 一支有動態螢幕、能連線應變中心的號誌 —— 那代表成本高,而且壞了不好修。我負責的是號誌本體: 怎麼讓它裝得上去、打得開、換得掉,而不用把路口封起來。

01

Start 起點

The brief added a dynamic screen and a bluetooth link to an emergency centre. Both push the cost up, and both have to survive weather. So the first thing I drew was not the shape — it was a lift mechanism and a set of modules, so the expensive parts could be carried up, clipped in and taken down again without rebuilding the pole. 功能上要有動態螢幕、要藍芽連線應變中心, 成本一定會高,而且都得防水。所以我第一個畫的不是造型, 是升降系統和模組 —— 讓貴的零件可以吊上去、扣上、再拆下來, 不必動到整根柱子。安裝、拆卸、修繕都會更快。

02

First build 第一版

The panel splits into four pieces — two on each face — with a three-colour bar along the top. Four small parts instead of one large one: cheaper to make, and a damaged section can be swapped on its own. 面板分成四塊組合,正反各兩塊, 上方放三色燈條。四塊小的取代一塊大的 —— 製作便宜, 壞掉也只要換壞掉的那一塊。

03

Centre 應變中心

The signal reports to a building. The three of us and our advisor kept trading the parts we each owned — you draw yours, I say what breaks when it meets mine, we both change something. 號誌要回報給一棟建築。 我們三個組員和指導教授會互相討論各自負責的項目 —— 你畫你的,我講它接到我這邊會出什麼問題,兩邊都改。

04

Second pass 第二版

Then I turned the three-colour bar into a screen as well. A signal that can only say stop and go can only say stop and go; a screen can say roadworks, crash, ambulance coming. I also put manual controls and an electronic lock on the pole so a technician can open it on site, and merged a street lamp into the body — a black pole at night is hard to see, which is a strange problem for a traffic signal to have. 後來我把上方的燈條也改成動態螢幕。 只有紅黃綠就只能講停和走;有螢幕就能顯示路障、車禍、救護車。 柱體上加了手動控制按鈕與電子鎖,師傅到現場打得開; 也把路燈結合進去 —— 黑色的柱子在夜裡不好看見, 那對一支號誌來說是很奇怪的問題。

05

Colour 視覺

We started with the white and orange of the high speed rail, because it looked calm. Then we changed it to red, yellow and green — the three colours everyone already reads without being taught. The subject was already the answer. 主視覺一開始用高鐵的白橘,因為看起來乾淨。 後來改成紅黃綠 —— 那三個顏色不用教,每個人都讀得懂。 題目本身就是答案。

06

Making it 做出來

Printed by a shop in China a senior recommended; two weeks in transit. What arrived was rough. I spent about a week sanding it by hand at a paint shop near school, handed it to the guy there to spray, then sanded it again after. The render took an afternoon. This took a month. 找學長推薦的大陸 3D 列印公司做, 寄過來花了兩週。拿到是粗的。我在學校附近的噴漆工廠用手打磨大約一週, 再給工廠的大哥噴漆,噴完繼續打磨才完成。 渲染圖畫一個下午,這個花了一個月。
