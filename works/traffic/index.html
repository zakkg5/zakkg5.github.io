2025 · Product · Graduation project

New Era Traffic System 新型態交通號誌

A traffic signal with a dynamic display and a link to an emergency centre. That makes it expensive, and awkward to service on a live road. My part was the signal itself: how to build it so one crew can put it up, open it and swap a part without closing the junction. 一支有動態螢幕、能藍芽連線應變中心的號誌,成本一定會高,材質也要防水。我負責的是號誌本體 —— 怎麼裝得上去、打得開、換得掉。

01

Start 起點

The brief added a dynamic screen and a bluetooth link to an emergency centre. Both push the cost up, and both have to survive weather. So the first thing I drew was not the shape. It was a lift mechanism and a set of modules, so the expensive parts could be carried up, clipped in and taken down again without rebuilding the pole. 紅綠燈會有動態螢幕、藍芽連線應變中心的功能,成本一定會高,也考慮防水材質。於是製作升降系統配合模組組裝,去減少成本、加速安裝與拆卸,修繕也會更快。

02

First build 第一版

The panel splits into four pieces, two on each face, with a three-colour bar along the top. Four small parts instead of one large one: cheaper to make, and a damaged section can be swapped on its own. 面板分成四塊組合,正反各兩塊,上方放置三色燈條。

03

Centre 應變中心

The signal reports to a building. The three of us and our advisor kept trading the parts we each owned. You draw yours, I say what breaks when it meets mine, we both change something. 配合紅綠燈的應變中心外型設計。組員三人與指導教授互相討論整體負責的項目,互相給予想法並更改。因為時間因素,以這一版去參加紅點與 iF。

04

Second pass 第二版

Then I turned the three-colour bar into a screen as well. A signal that can only say stop and go can only say stop and go; a screen can say roadworks, crash, ambulance coming. I also put manual controls and an electronic lock on the pole so a technician can open it on site, and merged a street lamp into the body. A black pole at night is hard to see, which is a strange problem for a traffic signal to have. 上方燈條也改成動態螢幕,增加設計感與更多的燈號變換,這樣號誌還能顯示路障、車禍、救護車等功能。柱體上增加手動控制的按鈕與電子鎖,方便修繕。還結合路燈,避免黑色在夜晚不易看見的問題。

05

Colour 視覺

We started with the white and orange of the high speed rail, because it looked calm. Then we changed it to red, yellow and green. Those are the three colours everyone already reads without being taught. The subject was already the answer. 號誌視覺的規劃與案例圖,以及箭頭導引的設計。主視覺顏色從高鐵的白橘變成紅黃綠 —— 直接表達主題。

06

Making it 做出來

Printed by a shop in China a senior recommended; two weeks in transit. What arrived was rough. I spent about a week sanding it by hand at a paint shop near school, handed it to the guy there to spray, then sanded it again after. The render took an afternoon. This took a month. 新型號誌是找大陸一家學長推薦的 3D 列印公司,寄過來耗時兩週。拿到成品後自己在學校附近的噴漆工廠用手打磨大約一週,再給工廠的大哥噴漆,噴完繼續打磨才完成。

07

Shown 展覽

The display cabinet was ours too, built at a woodwork and paint shop rather than ordered in. 展櫃也是我們自己的 —— 在漆木工那邊做的,不是叫現成的。
